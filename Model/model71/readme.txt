Model71: clean-history process context for a conditional LightGBM voter.

Models66 and 69 added online dependence features to the strong Model62
LightGBM and failed their pilots. Model71 instead adds 18 history-only
descriptors, repeated at each online step. They include a coefficient-of-
variation-style mean-to-scale gate, robust median-to-MAD ratio, distribution
shape, four autocorrelations, differenced volatility and volatility memory,
tail frequency, trend, half-history drift, and spectral power/entropy. These
describe what kind of stochastic process the online detector is watching;
they do not themselves inspect the online segment or future observations.
The earlier offline competition's reported coefficient-of-variation gate is
motivation only; the real-time task differs and this adaptation must be tested.
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/crunchlabhq_structural-breaks-sit-at-the-intersection-activity-7399127477681504258-88ue

The 317 Model62 detector-plus-whitened features, exactly 400,000 real and
400,000 aligned-positive synthetic rows per held fold, 40% synthetic mass,
LightGBM parameters, donor exclusions, five series-disjoint folds and Model39
stack/postprocess remain fixed. Only the 18 causal history-context columns
change. Real and synthetic history fingerprints are generated by the same
code, with prefix parity tests and resumable checksummed caches.

Four voter blends are prespecified: new-voter fractions 0.50/1.00 and
replacement weights 0.50/1.00. Pilot folds 0-1 need mean gain >=+0.0001
versus Model62 before confirmation folds 2-4 train. Promotion requires
confirmation and full held-series OOF TSAUC >=0.640, confirmation gain
>=+0.0005, at least 2/3 wins, positive paired-series bootstrap lower 95%
bound, and positive gains in both fixed ID halves. Scores are exploratory
because inherited feature selection and ensemble choices used development
data. The sealed outer holdout and competition test labels remain closed.

CPU only, four LightGBM threads, 15-hour local timeout, fold checkpoints.
No automatic packaging, upload or cloud submission.
