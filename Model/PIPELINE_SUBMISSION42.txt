1. WHAT THE MODEL IS (three parts, blended on z-scores)
   score = z(chain) + 1.5 * z(student) + 1.0 * z(nets)
   Weights come from the Model148 protocol (pilot folds 0-1 choose, folds 2-4
   confirm), last re-checked in Model171 with these exact components.

   a) CHAIN - unchanged since Submission28. 31 detector-bank LightGBM models +
      5 core voters + ExtraTrees + RandomForest -> 34 primitive probabilities ->
      a logistic stack ("m9") -> rolling max over 128 steps -> 10% of a compact
      154-feature model ("m11").  Serving: solution73.py, solution26.py,
      primitive_runtime.py, compact_runtime.py.  TRAINED ON DEVELOPMENT SERIES
      ONLY (the one part never given all-series training).
   b) STUDENT - the strong part. LightGBM, 317 causal columns + 27 split-test
      columns, trained on soft targets 0.5*y + 0.5*oracle-teacher, on ALL 10,000
      labelled series, with 40% of the weight on synthetic rows: the 1,000
      deployed splices per fold plus THREE pooled re-cut sets ("", _s148, _s149).
      Offline: models/model154/run_all.py arm AP1.  Serving: solution147.py +
      split_tests_numpy.py.
   c) NETS - 5 GRUs (hidden 256, 2 layers, one seed x five folds), 102 inputs
      (61 whitening + 40 detector columns + log1p t), trained on all 10,000
      series with the same oracle soft targets.  Offline: models/model170
      (build_all_sequences.py then models/model172/train_gpu2.py on a rented
      GPU).  Serving: gru_numpy.py, solution124.py.

4. HOW TO JUDGE A CHANGE (do not skip this)
   - Dev folds are ~0.006 OPTIMISTIC: 100+ models were selected on them.
   - The sealed outer holdout (1,667 series) matches the cloud within ~0.0014,
     and is the go/no-go test - EXCEPT for the nets and the student since
     Model154/170, which train on the holdout series, so for those judge on the
     dev folds and the public score.
   - Rule for any arm: gain > 2 standard errors AND >= 4 of 5 folds.
   - Precedent for why the rule is strict: Submission34 gained +0.0013 on dev,
     5/5, and LOST 0.0009 on the holdout.
   - Any single-fold gain above about +0.01 is a suspected LEAK. One was found
     on 2026-09-25: spliced series tagged by plan index pass the "not my fold"
     test for every plan but their own (see models/model172/readme.txt and
     split_splices.py).
