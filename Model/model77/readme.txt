Model77: use every row from the frozen aligned-splice pairs.

Model62 trains its best 317-feature LightGBM on 400,000 real rows and a
random 400,000-row subset of 566,000-583,000 causal rows generated by 1,000
real prebreak-to-real postbreak splice pairs per fold. Model77 keeps the same
donor pairs, exclusions, real rows, features, LightGBM recipe, seeds and 40%
synthetic weight mass, but uses every generated synthetic row. This raises
synthetic coverage by roughly 42% without increasing its total influence;
each synthetic row simply receives less weight.

The new full-splice candidate replaces either one half or all of Model62's
50% allocation inside Model67, leaving Model63 and Model64 at 25% each.
Those two replacement choices are frozen before fitting. Folds 0-1 form the
pilot and require at least +0.0001 mean TSAUC. If the gate passes, folds 2-4
are trained and promotion requires full and confirmation TSAUC >=0.640,
confirmation gain >=0.0005, 2/3 wins, a positive paired-series bootstrap
lower 95% bound, and positive gains in both fixed series-ID halves.

Feature caches are resumable and checksummed. All features use only clean
history and the current online prefix; donors exclude both the held series
fold and its adjacent stopping fold. Inherited parents and splice selection
remain development-informed, so this is exploratory held-series OOF. The
sealed outer holdout and competition labels stay closed. CPU only; no cloud
packaging, upload or submission is automatic.
